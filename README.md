## Sevda Anefi

Independent European VC researcher and writer; former software engineer, founder and operator. Based in Bucharest, pursuing Analyst/Associate roles at European and UK pre-seed to Series A funds.

I research European software and AI through product and technical evidence, buyer incentives, workflow adoption and market structure. My working thesis concerns unavoidable operational work: who owns the budget, why change is urgent, and whether a product can become durable infrastructure.

My background combines J.P. Morgan securitised-products origination, Morgan Stanley sales-and-trading summer training, production software engineering at DAZN, Funding Circle and Duffel, and founder/operator work at Zemedy, Fluento and Khaleea Jewelry.

At my family's jewellery retailer, I managed ERP implementation alongside e-commerce and brand work. I negotiated custom per-gram gold pricing with the implementer and trained staff. That experience shapes how I examine implementation burden and adoption. Precise rollout coverage and numerical outcomes are not established here.

Romania and CEE are a developing sourcing lens within Europe. My research and code are independent proof of work; I have no institutional VC investing track record or demonstrated proprietary deal access.
