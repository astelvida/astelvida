## Sevda Anefi

European AI investor-operator. London / Bucharest.

I underwrite companies where the software has to **enforce, write back, or evidence** something a regulated buyer is personally accountable for. Two theses, both with published falsifiers:

- **Governed Agentic Ops** — cross-stack runtime control, agent identity, and inspection-grade audit evidence. *The brake.*
- **Vertical System-of-Record AI** — authoritative write-back inside a regulated workflow. *The book of record.*

Above both: **Governed Systems of Record**, where removing the company breaks the workflow *and* the audit.

Before this: structured credit at J.P. Morgan and Morgan Stanley, production engineering at DAZN, Funding Circle and Duffel, three founder seats, and six months taking a 200-person CEE jewellery retailer off paper ledgers onto a live ERP. That last one is where the thesis comes from: **digitalisation before automation.**
